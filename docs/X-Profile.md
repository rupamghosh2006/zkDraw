## Content Templates

Modelled on the voice and structure of the four posts above.

| Post type | Cadence | Opening formula | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feature drop** | Per new feature | Action question or imperative | "Ready to run your own zkDraw? 🎲" |
| **Teaser** | Pre-launch / pre-update | Tension pair (private vs public) | "Your ticket stays private. The result doesn't." |
| **Explainer** | Monthly | Open question → answer | "What if a lottery could keep your pick private…" |
| **Verifier highlight** | After notable draws | Challenge to authority | "Don't trust the operator. Verify the draw. 🔍" |
| **Milestone / BIP** | On event | 1 data point + evidence asset | "Draw #42 settled on Preprod. 10 tickets. All 4 checks passed." |
| **Thread** | Monthly | Question that deserves 5–8 replies | "Why can't the lottery creator cheat? 🧵" |

**Formatting rules (derived from live posts):**

- Short sentences. Line breaks between ideas. No walls of text.
- Lead with the *so what* — never open with "In cryptography…" or "Today we…"
- One link per post, pointing to the most specific URL (e.g. `/create`, `/verify`, not the homepage).
- Always end with `#MidnightNetwork #ZK`. Add `#BuildInPublic` for dev progress posts.
- Attach a screenshot or recording to every post — media consistently outperforms text-only.
- Always tag `@MidnightNtwrk` when the network is mentioned inline.
